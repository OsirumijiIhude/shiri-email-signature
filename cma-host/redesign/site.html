1986The year our story began.

We are CM&A, a fully Zimbabwean-owned advertising and marketing agency. A team of thinkers and makers bringing strategy, creativity and communication together.

A little more about us

We first opened our doors in 1986. A merger with Cameron McKay & Finch in 1987 established CM&A Advertising & Marketing. Today, our home is Newlands, Harare.

Why we’re here

To help brands grow through communication that adds value, strengthens identity and delivers meaningful business results.

Where we’re going

To be known in Zimbabwe for strategic excellence, creative innovation and integrated communication that makes a difference.
